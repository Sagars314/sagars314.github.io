1About this policy

Saga StackBuilder (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Shopify application for vitamin, supplement and wellness stores. It lets merchants group existing products into goal-based bundles (“stacks”, for example a sleep stack or an immunity stack), offer those stacks to shoppers through a storefront widget, and apply a bundle discount at checkout, with the aim of increasing average order value.

This policy explains what personal and store data the App accesses, why, what it writes back to your store, how it uses artificial intelligence, how data is stored and protected, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights available to you. It covers the App and its associated services only. It does not cover Shopify itself, the merchant’s store, or any third-party website, each of which has its own privacy notice.

3Our role & whose data this covers

This policy concerns three groups of people and data:

  • Merchants. Shopify store owners and staff who install and use the App. For merchant account information, we generally act as a data controller.
  • Store catalog and order data. Product, variant and order information belonging to the merchant’s store, which the App caches and analyzes to provide its features. We process this on the merchant’s behalf, generally as a data processor (the merchant is the controller).
  • Storefront shoppers. Visitors to a merchant’s storefront who see or interact with the stack widget. The App records interaction events tied to an anonymous identifier, as described in Sections 4.4 and 9. We process this on the merchant’s behalf.

4.2 Cached store catalog data

To keep product pickers and the storefront widget fast, the App keeps a cache of your catalog: product titles, descriptions, handles, images, prices, availability, variants (including selling-plan and subscription indicators), product types and tags. Shopify remains the source of truth. The cache is kept current through product webhooks, and products you delete from Shopify are removed from it.

4.3 Order data (for attribution and plan limits)

The App receives order webhooks from Shopify (orders paid, orders cancelled, refunds created) and stores only the fields needed to attribute revenue to stacks and measure plan usage:

  • order ID and order number (for example #1001) and processing date;
  • line-item IDs, product references, quantities and amounts after discounts, and currency;
  • the stack markers the widget attaches to cart lines (Section 5);
  • the order’s sales channel (for example “online store”);
  • refund IDs and refunded or cancelled amounts;
  • whether the order is a test order.

We do not store customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, shipping or billing addresses, or payment details.

4.4 Storefront interaction events

On a merchant’s storefront, the widget records interaction events: when a stack is shown (impression), clicked, added to cart, or left after selection, plus the stack and products involved and where on the page it appeared. Each event is linked to an anonymous identifier: a random value (UUID) stored in the shopper’s browser localStorage under the key saga_sid. It persists across visits to the same store until the shopper clears their browser storage. It contains no name, email, address or other directly identifying information, and we do not store shoppers’ IP addresses or browser user-agent strings with these events. Section 9 explains when the identifier is and isn’t created.

The widget also stores two non-identifying layout values in localStorage (a cached card height and product handles) so it can render without visible jumps. The App sets no cookies.

The App also keeps aggregate, per-page counts of how often the widget was shown. These are not linked to any individual.

6Artificial intelligence

The App uses third-party large language models (LLMs) to help merchants build stacks.

6.1 What the AI does

When you set store goals, ask for stack suggestions, or when the App prepares stack templates for your catalog, it sends product information to a language model and receives back a suggested grouping, short benefit copy, and a rationale. The App also uses the model to review that copy and to suggest fixes to widget placement. These are drafts: nothing a model produces reaches your storefront until you review it and choose to activate it.

6.2 What is sent to the model

We send catalog and merchant-authored content only: product titles, descriptions, tags, product types and prices, plus the store goals and stack copy you write in the App. We do not send:

  • any customer or shopper personal data;
  • order data, order totals or purchase histories;
  • storefront interaction data or shopper identifiers;
  • your Shopify access token or any other credential;
  • your store’s account or contact details.

6.3 Training

We access models through providers’ paid APIs under terms that do not permit them to use API inputs or outputs to train their models. We do not train any model of our own on merchant data.

6.4 Caching

To keep the App responsive, we cache model output against a fingerprint of the catalog it was generated from. When your catalog changes materially, the cache is invalidated and suggestions are regenerated. Cached output is stored with your store’s data and deleted with it (Section 15).

6.5 Your control

AI output is a starting point, not a decision. You review and can edit every suggestion before it goes live, and you can build stacks entirely by hand without using the AI features.

9Storefront identifier & consent

The anonymous identifier in Section 4.4 is used for attribution. It stores no directly identifying details, but we treat it as subject to privacy and consent rules rather than claiming it is exempt.

The widget follows Shopify’s Customer Privacy API, which is how Shopify’s own cookie banner and most consent apps share a shopper’s choice:

  • Consent API present, analytics not allowed (declined or not yet answered): no events are sent and no identifier is written to the shopper’s browser.
  • Consent API present, analytics allowed: events are sent and the identifier is used.
  • No consent API on the store (no consent banner configured): events are sent and the identifier is used.
  • Any error while checking consent: no events are sent.

In every case the widget still works and can still add items to the cart; attribution simply undercounts when tracking is off.

Merchants are responsible for configuring a consent banner where their jurisdiction requires one (for example for shoppers in the EEA or UK) and for disclosing the App’s analytics in their own storefront privacy notice.

14Data security

We apply technical and organizational measures appropriate to the data we hold, including:

  • Access tokens: the copy of your Shopify access token held in our merchant records is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Shopify’s standard session storage, used to authenticate requests, also holds the token in our database, which is protected by restricted access controls.
  • Encryption in transit: HTTPS/TLS for all admin, API and storefront communication.
  • Verified requests: storefront requests are accepted only through Shopify’s signed App Proxy, and webhooks are verified with Shopify’s signature.
  • Per-merchant scoping: each merchant’s data is restricted to their own account.
  • Abuse protection: validation, ownership checks and rate-limiting on the public tracking endpoint.
  • Restricted administrative access to the production environment.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15Data retention & deletion

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy.

  • While the App is installed: we keep your account, settings, cached catalog, cached AI output and analytics data so the App works.
  • Deleted stacks: deleted stacks go to a Bin and can be restored for 30 days, after which they are permanently removed. Stacks that earned revenue are kept as a minimal record so your historical revenue figures stay accurate. “Delete forever” and “Empty bin” remove a stack’s analytics events immediately.
  • On uninstall: Shopify notifies us. We mark your store inactive, stop billing, delete your subscription record and session tokens, and remove the App’s discount setup. Your other data is kept until Shopify sends a shop-deletion request.
  • Shop deletion (shop/redact): Shopify sends this about 48 hours after uninstall. On receipt, we permanently delete all of that store’s data, including its account, stacks, settings, catalog cache, AI output, analytics, order attribution records, billing-usage records and sessions.
  • Customer redaction (customers/redact): for each order Shopify lists, we delete the order-linked analytics events and order attribution records. Billing-usage entries for those orders are kept with the order ID and order number replaced by anonymous placeholders, so plan-limit totals remain correct. Daily aggregate totals, which contain no order or customer identifiers, are kept.
  • Customer data requests (customers/data_request): we store no customer names, email addresses or contact details, so there is no customer personal data for us to provide.
  • Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for its standard log-retention period.

We may keep limited records longer where required for legal, accounting or security purposes, kept to the minimum necessary.
