Saga StackBuilder — Proof of Resolution

Implement Shopify Managed Pricing or the Shopify Billing API correctly

Both issues fixed and verified on the production app

Both issues raised in the review have been resolved and verified end-to-end on the production app (saga-stackbuilder-1), test store sk-labs-test.myshopify.com. The screenshots below follow a single uninstall → reinstall → subscribe cycle, in order. Each step is tagged to the issue it addresses.

Issue 1 Reinstall no longer keeps the previous plan active
Issue 2 Plan selection opens Shopify’s charge-approval page

Verification walkthrough

1

Starting state — a plan is active Issue 1

What this shows

Before the test, the store is on the Growth plan — the Growth card is badged “Current plan.” This is the active plan we expect the reinstall to clear.

2

Uninstall the app Issue 1

What this shows

The app is uninstalled from the store. On uninstall, the app clears its local subscription record so no stale plan can be restored on reinstall.

3

Reinstall — no plan is active Issue 1

What this shows

After reinstalling, the merchant lands on the billing page with no active plan. All three tiers show “Select,” and a “Subscribe to activate” banner appears. The previous Growth plan was not carried over — the merchant must choose a plan again.

4

App is gated until a plan is chosen Issue 1

What this shows

Attempting to open other pages (Dashboard, Stacks) redirects back to Billing. Access stays locked until the merchant selects and approves a plan.

5

Select a plan → Shopify’s charge-approval page Issue 2

What this shows

Selecting a plan routes directly to Shopify’s standard charge-approval page at admin.shopify.com/store/…/charges/… — with no shop-domain prompt. The flow stays inside the Shopify admin.

6

Approve → plan active, access restored Issue 2

What this shows

After approving, the merchant returns to the app with the newly selected plan badged “Current plan,” the subscribe banner gone, and full access to all pages restored.

Verification environment: Production app saga-stackbuilder-1, test store sk-labs-test.myshopify.com. Every step above was performed in the live Shopify admin.
